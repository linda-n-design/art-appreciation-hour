One artwork, one hour, shared with fellow art lovers.

Welcome

We look closely at a single artwork, spend time in quiet contemplation, and share what we observe and feel. Same artwork, different perspectives, over Zoom.

About

Art Appreciation Hour is inspired by Slow Art Day , a global annual event held in April where people meet at a museum or gallery and carefully look at art.

It’s also inspired by a community of practice I started and led at my last corporate job. Within three years, I organized and facilitated 80 Zoom meetups where colleagues around the world gathered for an hour of conversation and show-and-tell about AI. I loved the community-building and the conversations, so I wanted to do something similar, focused on the visual arts.

I feel that gathering with people who love art for art’s sake is so important to feeling good and finding meaningful connections with others and our creative spirits ❤️
